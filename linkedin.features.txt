1. Profile & Personal Branding
Professional Profile – Users can showcase their work experience, education, skills, and achievements.

Profile Customization – Add a professional photo, background image, summary, and featured content.

Skills & Endorsements – Users can list their skills and get endorsements from colleagues.

Recommendations – Colleagues and employers can write personalized recommendations.

2. Networking & Connections
Connection Requests – Users can send and accept connection requests to grow their professional network.

Follow Feature – Follow industry leaders, influencers, and companies without connecting directly.

People You May Know – AI-powered suggestions to connect with relevant professionals.

LinkedIn Groups – Join industry-specific groups to discuss topics and share insights.

3. Job Search & Recruitment
Job Listings – Search and apply for millions of jobs directly through LinkedIn.

Easy Apply – Apply for jobs with a single click using a LinkedIn profile.

Job Alerts – Get notified about new job postings that match user preferences.

LinkedIn Salary Insights – View salary ranges based on job title, location, and industry.

Company Pages – Learn about companies, their culture, and job openings.

Recruiter Access – HR professionals can find and contact potential candidates.

4. Content Sharing & Engagement
Posts & Articles – Share updates, write articles, and engage with professional content.

Reactions & Comments – Like, share, and comment on posts and articles.

News Feed – Personalized content feed based on interests and network activity.

Hashtags & Topics – Follow hashtags to stay updated on industry trends.

5. Learning & Development
LinkedIn Learning – Online courses on business, technology, and creative skills.

Certifications & Badges – Earn and showcase course completions and professional certifications.

AI-Powered Skill Assessments – Take tests to validate skills and display badges on profiles.

6. Messaging & Communication
Direct Messaging (InMail) – Private messaging between LinkedIn users.

Voice & Video Calls – Users can start voice or video conversations within LinkedIn.

Smart Replies & AI Suggestions – AI-powered reply suggestions for quick communication.

Event Invitations – Create and invite users to professional events.

7. Business & Marketing Features
LinkedIn Pages – Companies can create pages to build brand presence.

LinkedIn Ads – Targeted advertising for businesses, including job postings and sponsored content.

LinkedIn Sales Navigator – A tool for sales professionals to find and engage with potential clients.

Product Showcase – Companies can highlight their products and services.

8. Premium & Paid Features
LinkedIn Premium – Subscription plans for advanced job searching, networking, and insights.

InMail Credits – Paid users can send messages to people outside their network.

LinkedIn Recruiter – Advanced tools for recruiters to find top candidates.

LinkedIn Sales Navigator – Lead generation and CRM integration for sales teams.
